# OrbitMesh Home FAQ

**Document version:** 1.0
**Published:** 2026-06-01

Common questions about the OrbitMesh home R1 and N1 system. For detailed procedures, follow the linked product guides.

**Which power adapter should I use?**
Use the supplied 12 V power adapter. Do not substitute an improvised or third-party supply.

**How many nodes can I add?**
A home network uses one R1 router and supports up to five N1 nodes.

**Do I pair a node before running an Ethernet cable to it?**
Yes. Pair the N1 wirelessly first, then connect its Ethernet backhaul.

**Can I plug an N1 straight into my modem?**
No. An N1 connected directly to the modem or ONT sits outside the OrbitMesh network and will appear offline. An R1 is always required.

**What does the warranty cover?**
Hardware has a limited warranty against manufacturing defects for two years from the original retail purchase, subject to proof of purchase and inspection. We cannot confirm coverage in chat; support determines eligibility.

**What should I never share with support?**
Never share your full serial number, Wi-Fi password, or account password. Only the final four characters of the serial number are needed in chat.

**What does a factory reset do?**
A factory reset erases the network name, password, node pairings, and all local configuration. Every node must be paired again. Treat it as a last resort.

**Some of my smart-home devices cannot join the network. Why?**
WPA3-only mode may block older devices. Use WPA2/WPA3 transitional mode when an older device needs WPA2.

**When do firmware updates install?**
OrbitMesh normally installs updates overnight. You may also start an available stable update manually from the app.

**Where do I get help if these steps do not work?**
Use the support channel in the OrbitMesh app and provide your model, firmware version, LED state, and the steps you have already tried.
