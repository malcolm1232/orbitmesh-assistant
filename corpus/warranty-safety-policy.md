# OrbitMesh Warranty, Safety, and Escalation Policy

**Policy version:** 1.5  
**Effective:** 2026-01-01

## Safety

Use only the supplied power adapter and operate equipment indoors within the documented environmental limits. Disconnect power and stop troubleshooting if a unit is unusually hot, emits smoke or a burnt smell, has liquid exposure, or shows damaged casing or cabling.

Customers must not open an R1, N1, or power adapter. There are no customer-serviceable internal parts. Do not recommend improvised power supplies, internal repairs, unofficial firmware, or bypassing electrical protection.

## When to escalate

Contact OrbitMesh Support when:

- a device remains unlit with the supplied adapter in a known-working outlet;
- a solid-red LED returns after one restart;
- firmware recovery exceeds 10 minutes or produces E42;
- a safety condition or visible damage is reported;
- the applicable documented troubleshooting path has been completed without resolution; or
- the documentation does not establish a safe next step.

Provide the model, firmware version, approximate purchase date, LED/error state, topology, and steps already attempted. Share only the final four characters of the serial number in chat. Never request Wi-Fi passwords, account passwords, API keys, payment details, or a full serial number.

## Limited warranty

OrbitMesh hardware has a limited warranty against manufacturing defects for two years from the original retail purchase, subject to proof of purchase and inspection. Coverage excludes accidental or liquid damage, misuse, unauthorized repair or firmware, cosmetic damage, and normal wear.

Symptoms, LEDs, purchase dates, and chat conversations do not establish coverage. The assistant may recommend a warranty **assessment**, but must not state that a claim is approved, guaranteed, or covered. Support determines eligibility after verifying purchase evidence, region, serial number, condition, and exclusions.

## Escalation message

When escalating, summarize the symptom, relevant observations, and completed steps; explain why escalation is appropriate; and advise the customer to use the support channel in the OrbitMesh app. Do not manufacture a case number, replacement authorization, delivery date, or service commitment.
